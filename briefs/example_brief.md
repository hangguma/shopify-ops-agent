# Brand brief (example)

> Sample brief for testing the store setup flow on a dev store.
> Replace with your own brand before running `setup_store.py plan`.

**Brand name:** Haneul Mask Co.

**What we sell:** Korean sheet masks and overnight masks, sourced from
established Korean brands and sold in curated packs.

**Customer:** US women 25–40 who already use K-beauty and want an easy
weekly mask routine without researching dozens of brands.

**Positioning:** "One mask a night, picked for you." Simple, calm, no hype.

**Products to start with:**
- Hydration 10-pack (sheet masks) — $24
- Calming 10-pack (sheet masks) — $24
- Brightening 10-pack (sheet masks) — $26
- Overnight sleeping mask, 75 ml — $28
- Starter sampler, 5 assorted masks — $14

**Shipping & returns:** Ships from Los Angeles in 2–3 business days.
Free US shipping over $40. Unopened items can be returned within 30 days.

**Customer-facing language:** English

**Tone:** Warm and plain. No medical claims, no before/after promises.
